# Privacy Policy for SoutNaqi (صوت نقي)

**Last Updated:** September 28, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Thank you for choosing **SoutNaqi (صوت نقي)** ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. 

SoutNaqi is an open-source, on-device audio and video processing application built with privacy as a fundamental principle. 

## 2. Data Collection and Usage

**We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data or media files.**

- **Completely Offline Processing:** All audio and video processing, including AI vocal separation using the Demucs AI model, happens **entirely offline on your device**. 
- **No Cloud Servers:** We do not upload your audio recordings, videos, or processed media to any external servers or cloud services.
- **No Analytics or Tracking:** We do not integrate third-party analytics or tracking SDKs that monitor your usage behavior.

## 3. Device Permissions

To function correctly, SoutNaqi requires specific permissions on your device. We use these permissions strictly for the app's core functionality:

- **Storage / Photos / Media:** Required to import audio and video files from your device for processing, and to save the final exported files back to your device.
- **Microphone & Camera (if applicable):** Required only if you choose to record audio or video directly within the app for processing. We do not record or access the microphone or camera in the background.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the app is completely offline and does not connect to our servers to fetch policy updates, we advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will be posted on this page and are effective immediately upon publishing.
